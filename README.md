<h2 align="center">👋 About Arta</h2>

<table align="center" width="92%">
<tr><td>

I'm a **Python developer and AI builder** who cares more about *what a tool does for someone* than how clever the code looks. My work sits at the intersection of **backend engineering, applied AI, and automation** — turning repetitive, manual, or messy processes into small, dependable pieces of software.

I started with C++ and systems-level thinking, which shaped how I approach every project since: **build the structure right first, then make it smart.** That's why my Python tools stay lean and my AI integrations stay practical rather than gimmicky — a bot that reliably saves someone an hour a day beats a flashy demo that breaks in production.

Right now I'm deep in **FastAPI and Flask backends, LLM-powered assistants (OpenAI & Gemini), and Telegram automation** — infrastructure that quietly does its job in the background. Longer term, I'm growing into **backend architecture and system design**, because what I want to build next needs to scale beyond a single script.

**What drives me:** the moment a tool goes from *"this could work"* to *"this just saved me real time."*

</td></tr>
</table>
